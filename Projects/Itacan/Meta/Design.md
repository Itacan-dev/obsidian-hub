
## Icons

For icons, Itacan will mostly rely on Lucide.

Lucide is good because of its
- **Familiarity:** users recognize common actions immediately.
- **Consistency:** icons share one coherent visual language.
- **Coverage:** a large set handles most UI needs without reinventing basics.
- **Maintainability:** fewer custom assets to design, document, and update.
- **Polish:** professionally drawn, scalable SVG icons with reliable rendering.
- **Speed:** faster product development and easier iteration.
- **Accessibility through convention:** standard symbols reduce interpretation effort.

But to stick out and have identity, we'll need custom made icons.

We definitely will need custom-made icons for
- the app logo
- The Oath
	- The Oath itself
	- Oathbound mode
	- Oath fulfilled
	- Oath broken
- Atlas (the browser)
- Athena (the AI assistant)
- Workspace
- Presets
- The progress panel

We maybe will want custom-made icons for
- The four resources (Knowledge, Research, Explorations, Inspiration)
- Projects so they stick out as "Itacan projects"
