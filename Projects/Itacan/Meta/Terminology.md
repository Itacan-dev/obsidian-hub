# Terminology

This document defines the core terminology used throughout the Itacan project.

The naming follows the theme of **The Odyssey**: a journey of exploration, knowledge, and returning with greater understanding.

## Itacan

**Itacan** is the name of the overall application and the user's personal knowledge environment.

The homepage and central workspace are called **Itacan**. It represents the user's own island — a place of organization, reflection, preparation, and creation.

## Atlas

**Atlas** is the integrated browser within Itacan.

Named after the figure who carries the world, Atlas represents exploration of the outside world. It is based on the original Zenith browser project and provides a controlled gateway to external information while remaining connected to the user's personal knowledge space.

## Athena

A system Ai integration that guide the user.
