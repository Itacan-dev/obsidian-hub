## Recovery boundary

Uncommitted preparation can be abandoned without changing existing repositories. After a source enrollment/upgrade commit, there is no automatic downgrade; preserve exact stronger barrier and retained-request evidence. Recover lost acknowledgement by inspecting receipts or explicitly replaying the identical safe transition.

**First publication is the external rollback boundary.** Once another device may have consumed evidence, restoring a stale repository snapshot is unsafe. Recover forward: keep all identities and immutable proofs; reconcile original retained commands, re-export committed evidence, wait for missing dependencies/locations, or review and resolve eligible conflicts. A writer-proven terminal rejection ends only that exact operation; a later attempt requires fresh review and a new ID. Uncertain publication never qualifies from receipt absence alone. Do not delete fences, force-unlock writers, overwrite immutable objects or fabricate a replacement genesis.
